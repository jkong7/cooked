# Launch plan

Inputs: the Reels feed research (`~/dev/reels-research`, 2026-09-25) and the YouTube comment-rate study (852 videos, https://claude.ai/artifact/94gg2gS9VSJLkt2itTR1R7).

## What the data says

| Finding | What cooked does with it |
| --- | --- |
| The comment fights are the product: height, dating, money and class posts pull 100–400 reply threads | Every debate *is* the reply fight, with a winner |
| Pushback is only 0–7% of top comments; real disagreement lives in replies | Matchmaking guarantees an opponent on the other side |
| Two-sided, specific, identity-relevant claims beat "why…" framings (free will 1.58% comment rate, being single ~1%) | Takes are short declarative claims: "5'10 is short.", "Being single past 25 is a choice." |
| Race comparisons get huge numbers and get accounts banned | Excluded from the library and blocked in challenges |
| Hooks: one bold line of text on screen, then a reaction | The clip opens on the take in giant type |

## Content engine

Every finished debate is a ready-made vertical video. Seed the feed with:

1. **"I let an AI judge my argument"**: screen-record a debate against cookbot, end on the verdict slam and the roast line.
2. **Crowd flips**: clips where the tug-of-war bar swings in the last seconds.
3. **"Chud to Goat" series**: one creator climbing the tiers over a week, posting each verdict.
4. **Group-chat challenges**: "my friend said Marcus can't cook, so we settled it" with the challenge link in bio.
5. **Take of the hour**: post the live take as a text-on-screen reel with "argue it live" in the caption.

## Guardrails that keep it launchable

18+ gate, idea-not-identity prompts, pre-post moderation, reports, rate limits. Nothing in the marketing aimed at minors.
